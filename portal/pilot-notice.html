About this pilot

This is a test

The online application is being tested with a small group of Riara's international students before it's used for real applications. While the pilot runs:

  • Nothing is sent to Immigration. Submitting here does not apply for a Student Pass or any other pass.
  • Keep your real application going with Kelvin at the front office, the usual way.
  • Upload sample documents only — for example, a photo of a sheet of paper marked "TEST" for each item. Please don't upload your real passport or certificates.

What we collect

  • Your name, student email, student ID, country and (if you add it) phone number.
  • The files you upload, and the status of your test application.
  • Your ticks on the site's checklists, if you're signed in.

Who can see it

  • Only you, the International Student Representative and the front office (Kelvin).
  • Other students cannot see your account, files or application.
  • Files are stored privately and can only be opened through short-lived links by you or the office.

When it's deleted

All pilot accounts, applications and files will be deleted when the pilot ends. You can ask for your data to be deleted earlier by contacting the International Student Representative.

Questions or feedback

Contact the International Student Representative at internationalstudents@riarauniversity.ac.ke. Your feedback on what was confusing or didn't work is the whole point of this pilot — thank you for helping.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start my Student Pass application?

Start with the checklist on the Immigration page. It covers the documents, timelines, and steps you need before and after arrival.

What documents do I need before I arrive in Kenya?

Typically, you will need your admission letter, passport, visa or entry documents, accommodation details, and proof of funding or sponsorship. The exact list can vary by nationality and program.

Who should I contact for general questions?

Use the international students contact details listed at the top of this page, or email the representative directly for general support and guidance.

Can I get help with settling in after arrival?

Yes. The office and student community can help with practical matters such as housing, transport, banking, SIM cards, and getting connected to other students.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about your Student Pass? See the Immigration FAQs.

Who should I contact for general questions?

The International Student Representative — use the email or phone number above. For anything about your Student Pass application, contact Kelvin at the front office.

Can I get help with settling in after arrival?

Yes. The office and student community can help with practical matters such as housing, transport, banking, SIM cards, and getting connected to other students. See Living in Kenya.

How do I meet other international students?

Join the WhatsApp community and come to our events — orientation is a great place to start.

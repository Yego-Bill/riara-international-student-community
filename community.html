How it works

3

Connect and ask away

Ask questions, get settling-in tips, and meet other students before or after you arrive.

Community guidelines

  • Be respectful — this is a space for support, not spam or promotion.
  • Keep conversations relevant to student life, settling in, and mutual support.
  • For official Student Pass or immigration matters, contact the office directly rather than relying on group chat advice.
  • Report any concerns to the International Student Representative.
